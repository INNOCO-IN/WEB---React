Story

Every story is a point of light.

You have already been sensing MEWE — in a room, a decision, a moment you may not have named. Writing it is how that light reaches your own heart, and then someone else's. One ray is small; gathered, they don't stay many — they become a glow.

WHY WE GATHER STORIES

Every neighborhood is full of stories, untold.

A teacher who changed how her classroom breathes. A neighbor who knocked on a door.

A moment someone dared to try MEWE in real life — and whatever happened next.

We gather them here because none of us sees the whole picture alone. Every perspective is partial, so your story — exactly as you can tell it today — holds something the rest of us can't see without you. The well-crafted and the rough first telling are equally welcome. No spectators here — only people who notice, and pass it on.

What a MEWE story is

A moment where ME and WE turned out to be one.

Not a testimonial, not a polished essay. A moment you lived, noticed, were told, or can only imagine. In whatever form carries it: writing, a drawing, a photo, a song, a recipe.

What your story can be

Tell it however it arrives.

There is no wrong kind. And it can take any form — writing, a photo, a drawing, a song, a dance, even a recipe. However it wants to be told.

Something you lived

A moment you were inside. You acted, you were changed.

Something you noticed

MEWE you saw in the world: a performance, a street, a film, nature.

Something you were told

Someone else’s story, carried here by you.

Something you imagined

It hasn’t happened yet. A scene, a parable, a world you can picture.

Just start writing

A story is a free gift — it needs to be honest, not polished. Three honest sentences is a story.
