The moment

Never before have so many people been able to do so much.

Knowledge that once took a lifetime to reach now arrives in an afternoon. Tools that belonged to institutions now sit in our hands. A person in one country can learn alongside someone on the other side of the world, before lunch.

And yet the question of our time isn’t what we’re now capable of. It’s what we’ll do with it.

This is the moment IN was built for. Not to admire the capacity, but to practice with it — to turn enhanced capability into ordinary, daily acts of connection. A conversation started. A neighbor noticed. A story told so someone else can see further.

What we mean by protagonist

Not a leader, not an expert — simply someone who has decided not to watch.

Everyone who comes through IN walks their own path, at their own depth. Not ranks. Not requirements. Just how far you feel called to walk.

It starts with one free workshop. After that, four paths open — and each one leads back to the same place: a story, offered to the rest of us.

1
Explore MEWE

Participant

You’ve taken a workshop. Now you try it in the world: you initiate MEWE conversations with family, friends, and neighbors, and return to Open Studio to practice alongside others. Slowly it stops being an exercise and starts being how you live. And when you’re ready, you write your story and pass it on.

2
Learn by contributing

BridgeBuilder

An apprentice path, open on request. You start by identifying your own interest and purpose, then shadow real IN activities — supporting design, tech, storytelling, or facilitation, close to a mentor. You contribute to the growing body of stories, and you leave with genuine experience, a certificate, and a reference letter for whatever comes next.

3
A certified path

Facilitator (Pathfinder)

The one certified program at IN. Through the Pathfinder series you learn the workshop from the inside, practice with us, and keep returning to Open Studio as your own practice deepens. You earn the MEWE Facilitator certificate, then hold the space yourself — facilitating workshops in your own context, and sometimes creating new ones.

4
A full partner

IN-Collective

This one begins with a conversation: an application, an interview, an agreement. From there you’re part of the studio itself — developing workshops, co-facilitating, collaborating across the network, and hosting IN’s work in your own community, language, and culture. Independent, not a branch. Still in Open Studio with the rest of us.
