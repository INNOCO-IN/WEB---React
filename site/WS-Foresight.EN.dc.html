Two months on what is already alive in your neighborhood, and what it would look like if youth activated it.
Expand perspective
The second Global Lab of the Asia Exchange, on a two-month Action and Reflection cycle. It meets one root condition as a reality to encounter in the neighborhood, not a topic to study.
Issue addressed
The deeper barrier is not that youth lack capacity — it is that neither youth nor their neighbors believe youth can be protagonists. Foresight practice breaks this assumption by starting from what is already alive.
Design challenge
Identify one relational need in your neighborhood that no one is currently addressing — something emerging, something alive beneath the surface. Imagine what it would look like if youth activated it. Share your vision with two neighbors and document their response.
