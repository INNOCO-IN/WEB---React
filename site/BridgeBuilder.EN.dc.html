What is a BridgeBuilder?

A young person who senses that more is possible — and is willing to find out what that might look like.

In their neighborhood, in their community, in themselves. Not a leader with all the answers. Not an expert with a plan. A BridgeBuilder is someone who is curious enough to ask deeper questions, humble enough to listen carefully, and brave enough to try something — together with their neighbors.

What will you do?

Your journey unfolds over one year — and it begins not with a project but with a question about yourself.

You will move through three stages. Each one opens with a session in the same workshop series — the briefs below are examples of what those sessions look like.

Lab 1
Stage one

Going Beneath the Surface

What assumptions do I carry about the people around me? What keeps neighbors strangers to each other? You will practice conversations that go deeper than usual — and discover what is hidden beneath the visible surface of your neighborhood.

Along the way, you will connect with BridgeBuilders in other neighborhoods and other countries — sharing what you are learning, witnessing each other’s neighborhoods, and discovering that the longing for connection you feel is shared across cultures and borders.

What is a ME=WE Pop-Up Lab?

An artful invitation, not an event.

A space where neighbors encounter each other, perhaps for the first time, through creative expression, meaningful conversation, and shared action.

What it looks like depends on where you are and what your community needs. It might be a music gathering, a storytelling circle, a dialogue space, an intergenerational exchange, or something entirely new. The form is yours. The root system is shared.

Who can be a BridgeBuilder?

You — if you are a young person between 18 and 35 who:

Senses that something more is possible in your neighborhood

Is willing to begin with honest self-discovery

Can commit to one year of practice, reflection, and action

Wants to be part of something larger than one city or one country

You do not need prior experience in community work, facilitation, or social innovation. You need curiosity, sincerity, and a willingness to show up — for your neighbors, and for yourself.

What you will be part of

The beginning of a living ecosystem.

BridgeBuilders work in their own neighborhoods and stay connected to each other across countries. It is a careful attempt to create conditions for something that, if it takes root, will continue to grow and co-evolve long after any one year ends.

What you learn, what you document, and what you create becomes part of the ME=WE Neighborhood Archive — a living resource that others can build upon, in contexts and communities you may never meet but will have quietly touched.

The path is running now in the ME=WE Asia Exchange, with country teams in China, Japan, Korea, Nepal, and Taiwan — one example of where BridgeBuilders are at work.
