How a light gets here

1

Make something, or notice something. A few honest sentences, a drawing, a photo, a film, a song.

2

Share it through the Story form. One thing, one caption. Fifteen minutes is enough.

3

It appears here as a new light. Everything you add appears in the Constellation we are creating together. Some stories are also chosen for the Story page.

4

Add as many as you like. Each one joins your name's own small constellation.

Anyone can add — no workshop needed. And IN keeps adding too: every workshop ends with participants placing their pieces here. Many pieces begin in the Möbius Making workshop.
