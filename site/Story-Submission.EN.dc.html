If you're not sure where to start — four gentle beats
1The moment — where were you, and when?
2What happened — what you did, or what you noticed?
3What moved — in you, or between people?
4Why — why is this MEWE to you?

Three honest sentences is a story. Skip any beat that doesn't fit.
