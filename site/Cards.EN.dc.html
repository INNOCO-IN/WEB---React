Card reference · locked module + graphic set

Card system — three families

The structure — read this first
  • Three families, stable IDs — 1–6 Content cards (clickable, one destination each) · 7-1…7-6 Infographic panels (static, triangle in its own band) · 8-1…8-12 Mark cards (static, the mark's geometry as the plate) · 9-1…9-4 Quiet plates (cream watermark on the taxonomy plate, stands in for a missing photo). IDs are never renumbered; new variants append. A page-specific colouring is written 7-2 / Manifesto.
  • Image ratio = 16:9 on every photographic card. 4:3 is retired.
  • Family 7 band heights — three fixed steps only: S 160px · M 204px · L 248px. A row uses ONE step, never mixed. The variety shown below (7-1…7-6) demonstrates shape options, not height freedom.
  • Family 8 plates — fixed by position: top plate 190px · left column 152px wide · bottom plate 118px (cut-off diamond).
  • Type lock — text zone padding 28/28/32 · eyebrow Archivo 700/12px/0.16em (cream-safe colour values) · heading Newsreader 600/30px/1.05 · body 17px/1.3.
  • Rows — repeat(n, minmax(0,1fr)), 18px gap, stretch: every card in a row ends level.
  • Drift rules — 1–6 never ship without a destination · 7 and 8 never carry a tag rail or arrow · one height step per row.

Type 7 · Infographic panel — static, no destination · six triangles

The triangle now lives in its own band and no type ever sits on it — eyebrow, heading and body sit below on cream, so the shape can be as bold as it likes. Six numbered variants: side, size, proportion, count and colour all vary; only the two-zone structure is fixed. Call them by number — 7-1 through 7-6. In use, band height comes from the three fixed steps (S 160 · M 204 · L 248) — one step per row.

Rules — square corners, cream text zone, ink heading and body. Band height 88–132px and card width 300–420px are both free; a set should vary them — the shape is built at full height and cropped from the top, so what you see is its lower part. Triangle = one taxonomy colour on multiply over a second as the band — warm or cool, and multiply can be dropped whenever it would mute the second colour (Triangles 5 and 6). Eyebrow in a taxonomy value dark enough for cream. Heading Newsreader 600/30px, body 17px/1.3. No rail, no arrow, no link, no hover: diagrams, not doors.

Type 8 · Graphic statement card — the mark's shapes as the image

Shapes cut from the IN mark — cross, vee, triangle, diamond, then the same four ruled: cross, triangle and diamond gain a thin line along one edge, the vee a doubled band — each built from two to four translucent planes, multiplied where the overlap should darken. The plate replaces the photo — in the top zone by default, as a left-hand column or a closing band in the last two — and all type sits on cream, never on the shape, so the geometry can be vivid and the words stay easy to read. Every card carries its own number — 8-1 through 8-12. Nothing here is drawn as a solid object with a lit side: planes are flat and translucent, and the only place a new colour appears is where two of them cross. The overlap is the connection. Colour carries taxonomy; the shape carries the idea.

8-1Cross — two things held at once
Our Work
Practice, not talk

Workshops, projects with neighbours, the way we flip + connect — each one a way of practicing MEWE.

8-2Vee — many arriving at one
Our Posture
No fixed answers

We turn to nature, science, art, and social action for a common language, and co-create our way toward it.

8-3Triangle — one becoming a field
Our Shape
Not one centre

IN grows through shared practice — those who continue become IN-Collectives in their own communities.

8-4Diamond — where ME and WE overlap
Our Ground
Self is not separate

Personal growth and the wellbeing of the people around us were never two projects. That overlap is the whole idea.

8-5Cross, ruled — a thin line hugging one side
Our Method
Two lines, one path

Each arm keeps its angle and gains a thin rule along one edge — the same move, traced once more.

8-6Vee, doubled — thickening as it converges
Our Direction
Many, arriving

The doubled edge weights the vee toward its meeting point — more voices as the lines converge.

8-7Triangle crossed — graphic column beside the type
Our Aim
It points instead

A triangle turned on its side, crossed by an upright band. Only the crossing makes the third colour — the plate becomes a column and the type sits beside it.

8-8Diamond crossed — plate below the type
Our Overlap
Where the two meet

Reversed: the words come first and a diamond crossed by a level band closes the card — the overlap read as a conclusion rather than an opening.

8-9Meeting — two figures overlapping
Our Meeting
Two, overlapping

Two triangles come from opposite ends and share the middle. The overlap is the only new colour on the card — the connection makes it.

8-10Flip — a figure and its mirror crossing
Flip + Connect
Flip it, then cross

The same band flipped and laid over itself. Where the two pass through each other a third colour appears — the studio's whole method in one figure.

8-11Chain — a run of linked overlaps
Our Chain
Linked, not stacked

Three diamonds, each overlapping the next. Nothing sits on top of anything — every link is a shared edge, all the way down.

8-12Shade only — colour without a new shape
Our Depth
Three colours, one figure

The same shape laid three times in three colours, each whole and uncut. No new figure appears anywhere — the overlaps read only as a deeper shade, so what you see is depth of connection rather than another shape.

Rules — plate 190px on a 320px card by default; the last two variants move it, one to a left-hand column and one below the type. The zone can go anywhere except under the words. Two to four planes per shape. In the ruled variants a thin line traces one edge of the main plane, overlapping it on one side only; the vee instead carries a full doubled band. mix-blend-mode: multiply on any plane whose overlap should darken — dropped when it would mute the colour. Label 12px Archivo in a taxonomy colour dark enough for cream (amber becomes #7A5200, teal #146560, magenta #C21573). Title Newsreader 600/30px, body 17px/1.3. Non-clickable by default — add the Type 1 arrow only when the card has a real destination. Because the plate carries the palette, a page using these needs no colour divider bar.

Content fields · merged with the team's workbook

One row of the workbook fills one card. These are the columns the team is already writing into — the card types above are built from exactly these fields, so nothing has to be renamed on either side.

Field
What it does on the card
id
Stable handle — HOME/1, COMM/NEWS/3, PROJ/2. Also the image-slot key, so one drop fills the card and its destination page.
order
Position in its grid or feed. Rotate — don't accumulate.
uploaded
yes → photo card (Type 1/2/3). no → the no-photo build: 14px taxonomy bar (Type 4), date block (Type 5), or a graphic plate (Type 7/8). Never an empty frame.
type
The rail label — Community · News · Project · Story · Story · Writing · Tool. One line only; shorten rather than wrap.
eyebrow
Archivo 700/13px line above the title. On News and Event cards it carries full date · place.

body
One or two sentences, clamped to two lines on the card. Longer copy belongs in full story.
image
Path, e.g. community-img/mewe-hub.jpg. Blank is legitimate — see uploaded.
bg

Destination page. No dead cards: if the page doesn't exist yet, the arrow becomes a muted Page coming mark.

status
live ships · example is written but unconfirmed · template is a slot description, never published. Archived rows render in the inactive state.

credit_href

Colour follows type, so either those rows change section or green joins the taxonomy. One decision, then the sheet is the single source.

Family 9 · Quiet plate — what an empty photo zone shows

The mark's shapes as a watermark: cream planes at 11–16% opacity on the card's taxonomy plate. Quiet on purpose — it reads as texture from a distance and as the mark up close. A dropped photo covers it completely, so nothing needs removing later.

9-1Cross · deep blue
9-2Vee · magenta
9-3Triangle · orange
9-4Diamond · amber

Rules — always cream planes on the taxonomy colour, opacity 0.11 and 0.16, exactly two planes, 16:9 zone. Never coloured planes (that's family 8's job) and never on top of a photo. Shape rotates by card so neighbours differ: cross · vee · triangle · diamond.

The rules on this page
  • Tag rail — rotates 90° clockwise (reads downward), dot at the top, sitting 13px from the left edge, 21px from the top of the bottom zone. The label is always one line — never wraps to two; if a tag is too long, shorten the wording ("Story · Nature" → "Story"). On a colored card the dot matches the tag text color. On the two-zone Home module (paper-dim bottom) the dot is the card's taxonomy color — the one place that color appears below the image — with a 1px ink ring on the light tones (amber, yellow).
  • Type locked — tag Archivo 700/12px · eyebrow Archivo 700/13px (same color as tag) · title Newsreader 700/30px, line-height 1.0 · body Newsreader 400/17px, line-height 1.3.
  • Image — 16:9 frame, any photo auto cover-crops to fill. Identical shape on every card.
  • Spacing — fixed eyebrow → title → body gaps, same on every card. Equal card height; body clamped to 2 lines for a clean bottom edge.
  • Arrow — filled ink #2E3B40 circle, paper glyph, no border, bottom-right. Link sits on the arrow only so all text stays editable.
  • Corners square. Background = warm supporting tint of the page color; no two adjacent cards share a color.
  • Six types — (1) Content: one Topic tag. (2) Story: Topic on the rail + Format pictogram top-right of the image (Writing · Drawing · Photo · Video · Music · Dance). (3) News with photo: 4:3 image, eyebrow carries full date · place. (4) News without photo: image zone replaced by a 14px taxonomy bar — the default when no photograph exists. (5) Event: solid date block (day + month/year) in place of the image, for anything joinable. (6) Quote / insight: text only on a solid taxonomy field, arrow inverted.
  • Two static types — (7) Infographic panel: a triangle in its own band with the type on cream below — never over the shape. Six numbered variants (Triangle 1–6) vary side, size, proportion, count and colour; card width 300–420px and band height 160–240px are meant to differ across a set. (8) Graphic statement card: the IN mark's own shapes — cross, vee, triangle, diamond, each also ruled with a thin line along one edge — as the plate in place of a photo. Both are non-clickable: diagrams, not doors.
  • States — Active: full color, arrow enabled. Inactive/Archived: desaturated + muted, no arrow.
  • One row = one card — every card is filled from the team workbook's columns (id · order · uploaded · type · eyebrow · title · body · image · bg · link · feed · status · credit · full story). uploaded: no selects a no-photo build rather than leaving an empty frame; bg is always a colour name, resolved from the taxonomy; status: template never ships. See Content fields above.
