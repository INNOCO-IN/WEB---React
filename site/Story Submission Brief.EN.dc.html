Story Brief
Every story is a point of light.
You have already been sensing MEWE. Writing it is how that light reaches someone else.
Fifteen minutes is enough · innoco.co · Since 2015
What counts
Four kinds of story, all of them welcome.
A story is a free gift — it needs to be honest, not polished. You don't have to have run a workshop, or use the word MEWE at all.
Lived
Something you lived
A moment you were inside. You acted, and something in you changed.
Noticed
Something you noticed
MEWE you saw in the world: a performance, a street, a film, nature.
Told
Something you were told
Someone else's story, carried here by you. Say whose it is if you can.
Imagined
Something you imagined
It hasn't happened yet. A scene, a parable, a world you can picture.
How to tell it
Four gentle beats. Skip any that doesn't fit.
1
The moment
Where were you, and when? One line of ground is enough.
2
What happened
What you did, or what you noticed happening around you.
3
What moved
In you, or between people. This is usually the heart of it.
4
Why
Why is this MEWE to you? Your own words, not ours.
Three honest sentences is a story.
Form
Describe it in words first. Then bring whatever carries it.
A short description is the one thing we always need. If the story also lives in another form, tell us which and attach it — that is how we group and show it in the Constellation.

Required
Your story, and an email.
Two of the five steps. Everything else can be skipped.
Optional
Form, arc, and your name.
Leave the name blank to stay anonymous. Let IN place it on the arc.
What happens next
Points of light become one constellation.
01
Make or notice
You send it in
One thing, one caption. A few honest sentences, a drawing, a photo, a film, a song.
02
We read it
You get a note back
We place it on the ME=WE arc if you didn't. We may gently edit for length — never for meaning.
03
It appears
A new light in the Constellation
A virtual museum of neighbour expressions, built together. Some stories are also chosen for the Story page.
