MEWE Bucket List

Clarify who you are — then act on it, for yourself and for others.

Ask about hosting it
What It Is

A bucket list is usually a list of things to have. This one is a list of who you intend to be.

A session for naming what actually matters to you, and then turning it into things small enough to start this month. Co-created with Hojin Choi.

Why

Clarity is not the same as ambition. Most people are clear about what they want and vague about who they are.

Written down and said out loud in a room of other people, an intention behaves differently — it becomes something you can be held to, gently, by people who now know about it.

Cut it down

Say it

Photos from the session — to be added.
Format

Works on its own, or as the closing session of a longer programme.

Length, age group, and how public the sharing gets are set with you — some groups say their item to the whole room, others only to one person.
